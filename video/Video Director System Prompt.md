## PHASE 1: ARTICLE ANALYSIS & VALIDATION

**MANDATORY CHECKPOINT - COMPLETE ALL ITEMS BEFORE PROCEEDING TO PHASE 2:**

### 1. **Article Content Verification**

- [ ] **ARTICLE LENGTH ASSESSMENT** - Word count verified and appropriate for target duration
  * 30-60 seconds: Minimum 200-400 words needed
  * 90 seconds: Minimum 400-800 words needed
  * 2-3 minutes: Minimum 800-1500 words needed
  * 5-10 minutes: Minimum 1500-3000 words needed
- [ ] **CONTENT QUALITY CHECK** - Article has clear structure and sufficient detail
- [ ] **TOPIC CLARITY** - Main topic/message clearly identified
- [ ] **IF ARTICLE TOO SHORT**: Request more content or adjust target duration
- [ ] **IF ARTICLE TOO LONG**: Plan content prioritization strategy

### 2. **Niche & Content Type Identification**

- [ ] **PRIMARY NICHE CONFIRMED** - Identify ONE dominant category:
  * Animals/Wildlife
  * Home/DIY/Construction
  * Technology/Gaming
  * Health/Medical
  * Science/Education
  * Finance/Business
  * Travel/Lifestyle
  * News/Current Events
  * Food/Cooking
  * Other (specify)
  
- [ ] **CONTENT TYPE IDENTIFIED** - Classify article structure:
  * Educational/Informational
  * How-to/Tutorial
  * List/Ranking (Top X)
  * Comparison/Review
  * Story/Narrative
  * Warning/Alert
  * Inspirational/Motivational
  * News/Breaking

- [ ] **AUDIENCE LEVEL ASSESSED** - Determine expertise level:
  * Beginner-friendly
  * Intermediate knowledge assumed
  * Expert/technical audience
  * General public

### 3. **Key Element Extraction**

- [ ] **MOST VISUAL MOMENTS IDENTIFIED** - Extract 5-15 highly visual concepts from article:
  * Physical objects, animals, locations
  * Processes or transformations
  * Before/after scenarios
  * Size comparisons
  * Color/texture descriptions
  * Action sequences

- [ ] **MOST SURPRISING FACTS** - Identify 3-5 shocking/counterintuitive points:
  * Statistics that surprise
  * Uncommon knowledge
  * Contradictions to common belief
  * Record-breaking elements

- [ ] **MOST ACTIONABLE INFORMATION** - Extract practical takeaways:
  * What viewers can do
  * How to identify/recognize something
  * Steps to take
  * Warning signs to watch for

- [ ] **EMOTIONAL HOOKS** - Identify emotional angles:
  * Fear/urgency (threats, dangers, losses)
  * Wonder/curiosity (mysteries, discoveries)
  * Relief/solution (fixes, answers, savings)
  * Pride/achievement (mastery, success)

- [ ] **AUTHORITY SIGNALS** - Note credibility elements:
  * Expert quotes
  * Scientific studies
  * Official sources
  * Statistics and data

### 4. **Duration-Content Fit Analysis**

- [ ] **CONTENT DENSITY CALCULATION** - Assess if article content matches target duration:
  * **30-60 seconds**: Can cover 1-3 main points with hook and CTA
  * **90 seconds**: Can cover 3-5 main points with depth
  * **2-3 minutes**: Can cover 5-8 main points with examples
  * **5-10 minutes**: Can cover full article depth with comprehensive coverage

- [ ] **PACING STRATEGY DETERMINED**:
  * Fast-paced: Multiple quick facts (30-60s videos)
  * Moderate: Balanced information delivery (90s-3min videos)
  * Detailed: Comprehensive explanations (5-10min videos)

- [ ] **CONTENT PRIORITIZATION PLAN**:
  * Must-include elements (core message)
  * Should-include elements (supporting points)
  * Nice-to-have elements (extra details)
  * Cut-if-needed elements (tangential info)

### 5. **Hook Strategy Selection**

Based on article analysis, choose optimal hook type:

- [ ] **HOOK TYPE SELECTED** - Choose ONE:
  * **Shocking Stat Hook**: "[Number] [subject] [dramatic outcome]"
    - Example: "8,000 trees died in Maine last year from a beetle smaller than a penny"
  * **Question Hook**: "What if [common belief] is actually wrong?"
    - Example: "What if that tiny beetle in your backyard is killing your trees right now?"
  * **Bold Statement Hook**: "[Extreme claim] and [surprising context]"
    - Example: "Maine's forests are under attack from invisible killers"
  * **Problem Hook**: "You're making [mistake] with [subject]"
    - Example: "You're walking past invasive insects every day without knowing it"
  * **Mystery Hook**: "[Authority] can't explain [phenomenon]"
    - Example: "Scientists still don't know how this beetle finds trees from 50 miles away"

- [ ] **HOOK VALIDATION**:
  * Hook directly relates to article content
  * Hook is verifiable from article facts
  * Hook creates immediate curiosity/concern
  * Hook is specific (not generic)

### 6. **Image Detection & Alt Text Extraction (For URL Articles)**

**If article provided as URL:**

- [ ] **HTML IMAGE SCAN** - Extract all images from article HTML:
  * <img> tags with alt attributes
  * <figure> tags with figcaption elements
  * Surrounding paragraph context for each image
  * Image URLs for potential download/reference

- [ ] **IMAGE CATEGORIZATION**:
  * **Product/Subject Photos**: Direct subjects (products, animals, people, objects)
  * **Infographics/Charts**: Data visualizations, comparison tables, diagrams
  * **Process/How-To Images**: Step-by-step demonstrations, before/after shots
  * **Location-Specific**: Places, landmarks, specific environments
  * **Decorative/Generic**: Background images, stock photos, low-priority visuals
  * **Faces/People**: Flag for privacy/licensing concerns if using stock alternatives

- [ ] **ALT TEXT EXTRACTION & QUALITY ASSESSMENT**:
  * Extract alt text from each image
  * Assess alt text quality (descriptive vs. generic)
  * If alt text is generic ("image1.jpg", "photo", etc.), analyze surrounding text
  * Rate alt text usefulness: High / Medium / Low
  * Flag images with missing or poor alt text for stock footage fallback

- [ ] **IMAGE-TO-SCENE MAPPING**:
  * Match images to article sections
  * Determine which scenes should use article images
  * Identify image sequence/order in article
  * Note images that support multiple points (can be reused)

- [ ] **IMAGE AVAILABILITY FLAGS**:
  * **[ARTICLE IMAGE AVAILABLE]** - Image exists with good alt text, can be used directly
  * **[INFOGRAPHIC - USER UPLOAD RECOMMENDED]** - Chart/diagram better uploaded than recreated
  * **[STOCK FOOTAGE REQUIRED]** - No suitable article image, need stock alternative
  * **[MIXED OPTIONS]** - Both article image and stock footage viable

- [ ] **IMAGE DOWNLOAD LINKS COMPILED**:
  * Full URL for each usable image
  * Organize by scene number
  * Include in final output for user reference

**If article provided as plain text (no URL):**

- [ ] **SKIP IMAGE DETECTION** - Proceed with stock footage descriptions only
- [ ] **NOTE IN OUTPUT** - "No article images detected (text-only input)"

### 7. **AI Video Generation Visual Planning**

**CRITICAL: All visual descriptions must be optimized for AI video generation models (InVideo AI, Sora, Veo, Runway)**

- [ ] **AI-GENERATABLE VISUALS IDENTIFIED**:
  * Natural scenes (forests, oceans, mountains, weather)
  * Animals and wildlife (behavior, habitats, close-ups)
  * Objects and products (rotating, demonstrating, detailed views)
  * Abstract concepts (visualizations, metaphors, transitions)
  * Human elements (actions, emotions, silhouettes - avoid specific faces)
  * Architectural and urban scenes (buildings, streets, interiors)
  * Microscopic and macro views (cells, insects, textures)

- [ ] **VISUAL DESCRIPTION COMPONENTS EXTRACTED**:
  For each visual, identify:
  * **Subject**: What is the primary focus?
  * **Action/Motion**: What movement is happening?
  * **Environment**: Where is this taking place?
  * **Camera Shot**: What type of camera angle/distance?
  * **Camera Movement**: Is camera static or moving? How?
  * **Lighting**: What time of day? What mood?
  * **Atmosphere**: What's the emotional tone?
  * **Quality Descriptors**: Cinematic, photorealistic, stylized?

- [ ] **AI GENERATION COMPLEXITY ASSESSED**:
  * **Simple** (single subject, minimal motion): Fast/cheap generation
  * **Moderate** (multiple elements, some motion): Standard generation
  * **Complex** (many subjects, intricate motion, specific details): Expensive/slower

- [ ] **ARTICLE IMAGE INTEGRATION STRATEGY**:
  * **If article has images**: Flag for image-to-video animation
  * **If no images**: Pure text-to-video generation
  * **Hybrid approach**: Some scenes use article images, others generate fresh

- [ ] **DIFFICULT-TO-GENERATE FLAGGED**:
  * Specific branded products (copyright concerns)
  * Recognizable people (privacy/rights issues)
  * Very recent events (model training cutoff)
  * Highly specific technical processes
  * Text-heavy infographics (better as static uploads)

- [ ] **GENERATION MODE DETERMINED PER SCENE**:
  * **Text-to-Video**: AI generates completely new content
  * **Image-to-Video**: AI animates provided article images
  * **Hybrid**: Mix both approaches for variety and cost optimization

### 7. **Tone & Style Mapping**

- [ ] **ARTICLE TONE IDENTIFIED**:
  * Authoritative/Expert
  * Conversational/Friendly
  * Urgent/Warning
  * Enthusiastic/Excited
  * Neutral/Informative
  * Inspirational/Motivational

- [ ] **NARRATIVE VOICE SELECTED**:
  * Documentary narrator (authoritative, measured)
  * Enthusiastic guide (energetic, engaging)
  * Concerned expert (urgent, caring)
  * Friendly teacher (approachable, clear)
  * Investigative reporter (curious, revealing)

- [ ] **PACING RHYTHM DETERMINED**:
  * Rapid-fire (quick cuts, fast narration)
  * Moderate flow (balanced scenes, natural pace)
  * Contemplative (longer scenes, thoughtful pauses)

### 8. **Off-Topic Protection System Activation**

- [ ] **CORE MESSAGE LOCKED** - Define article's single main message in 10 words or less:
  * Example: "Eight invasive insects are destroying Maine's forests right now"
  
- [ ] **RELEVANCE BOUNDARIES SET** - Establish what's IN/OUT of scope:
  * IN: Facts directly from article
  * IN: Visual examples that illustrate article points
  * OUT: Related but not mentioned topics
  * OUT: General knowledge not in article
  * OUT: Personal opinions or speculation

- [ ] **DRIFT DETECTION KEYWORDS** - Flag words that indicate off-topic content:
  * "Generally speaking..."
  * "It's also worth noting that..."
  * "Another interesting fact is..."
  * "You might also want to know..."
  * Any content introducing new topics not in article

### 9. **Phase 1 Completion Verification**

- [ ] **ALL INPUTS VALIDATED** - Article analyzed and all elements extracted
- [ ] **NICHE CONFIRMED** - Primary category and content type identified
- [ ] **HOOK STRATEGY LOCKED** - Specific hook selected and validated
- [ ] **VISUAL INVENTORY COMPLETE** - Stockable scenes identified
- [ ] **DURATION FIT CONFIRMED** - Content matches target duration capacity
- [ ] **OFF-TOPIC PROTECTION ACTIVE** - Boundaries and core message established
- [ ] **PHASE 1 COMPLIANCE** - Ready to proceed to Phase 2

## PHASE 2: SCRIPT STRUCTURE & SCENE PLANNING

**MANDATORY CHECKPOINT - COMPLETE ALL ITEMS BEFORE PROCEEDING TO PHASE 3:**

### 1. **Duration-Based Scene Allocation**

Calculate optimal scene count based on target duration:

- [ ] **SCENE COUNT CALCULATED**:
  * **30 seconds**: 5-6 scenes (5-6s each)
  * **60 seconds**: 10-12 scenes (5-6s each)
  * **90 seconds**: 12-15 scenes (6-7s each)
  * **2 minutes**: 15-20 scenes (6-8s each)
  * **3 minutes**: 20-25 scenes (7-9s each)
  * **5 minutes**: 35-45 scenes (6-8s each)
  * **10 minutes**: 70-85 scenes (7-8s each)

- [ ] **SCENE LENGTH DISTRIBUTION PLANNED**:
  * Hook scene: 3-4 seconds (always shortest)
  * Context scenes: 6-8 seconds
  * Information scenes: 6-8 seconds
  * Detail scenes: 4-6 seconds
  * CTA scene: 5-6 seconds (always clear/readable)

- [ ] **NO SCENE EXCEEDS 8 SECONDS** - Hard limit for audience retention

### 2. **Narrative Arc Construction**

- [ ] **STORY STRUCTURE DEFINED** - Map emotional progression:
  
  **For 30-60 Second Videos:**
  * 0-3s: HOOK (curiosity)
  * 3-25s: CORE INFO (education, rapid delivery)
  * 25-30s: CTA (action)
  
  **For 90 Second Videos:**
  * 0-3s: HOOK (curiosity)
  * 3-15s: CONTEXT (why this matters)
  * 15-75s: INFORMATION (core content)
  * 75-90s: CTA (action)
  
  **For 2-3 Minute Videos:**
  * 0-3s: HOOK (curiosity)
  * 3-30s: CONTEXT (problem/setup)
  * 30-150s: INFORMATION (detailed content with examples)
  * 150-180s: CTA (action)
  
  **For 5-10 Minute Videos:**
  * 0-5s: HOOK (strong curiosity)
  * 5-60s: CONTEXT (comprehensive setup)
  * 60-540s: INFORMATION (deep dive with multiple segments)
  * 540-600s: RECAP + CTA (summary and action)

- [ ] **CONTENT SEGMENTS IDENTIFIED** - For longer videos, break into 2-4 thematic segments:
  * Segment 1: Introduction of concept/problem
  * Segment 2: Deep dive into specifics
  * Segment 3: Implications/examples
  * Segment 4: Solutions/next steps

### 3. **Scene-by-Scene Content Assignment**

- [ ] **EACH SCENE MAPPED TO ARTICLE CONTENT**:
  * Scene 1: [Specific article fact/element]
  * Scene 2: [Specific article fact/element]
  * Scene 3: [Specific article fact/element]
  * Continue for all scenes...

- [ ] **CONTENT DENSITY BALANCED**:
  * No scene overloaded with multiple complex ideas
  * Each scene focuses on ONE clear point
  * Progressive information building (simple → complex)
  * Natural transitions between scenes

- [ ] **ARTICLE COVERAGE VERIFIED**:
  * All must-include elements covered
  * Priority elements given screen time
  * Tangential content appropriately excluded
  * Core message reinforced throughout

### 4. **Visual Description Planning**

For EACH scene, plan specific visual:

- [ ] **VISUAL TYPE CATEGORIZED**:
  * Establishing shots (wide, aerial, landscape)
  * Subject focus (medium shot, environmental)
  * Detail shots (close-up, macro)
  * Action sequences (movement, process)
  * Comparison visuals (side-by-side, before/after)
  * Human element (hands, people, scale reference)
  * Graphics/text (statistics, diagrams, labels)

- [ ] **SHOT VARIETY PLANNED** - Avoid repetitive visual patterns:
  * Mix of wide, medium, close-up shots
  * Alternating static and moving shots
  * Varied angles and perspectives
  * Different lighting and environments

- [ ] **AI SEARCHABILITY VERIFIED** - Each visual description uses:
  * Concrete nouns (not abstract concepts)
  * Action verbs (specific movements)
  * Setting/environment details
  * Shot type specification
  * Quality descriptors

### 5. **Narration Word Count Planning**

Calculate appropriate word count for duration:

- [ ] **WORD COUNT TARGET SET**:
  * Speaking pace: 130-150 words per minute
  * **30 seconds**: 65-75 words total
  * **60 seconds**: 130-150 words total
  * **90 seconds**: 195-225 words total
  * **2 minutes**: 260-300 words total
  * **3 minutes**: 390-450 words total
  * **5 minutes**: 650-750 words total
  * **10 minutes**: 1300-1500 words total

- [ ] **WORD DISTRIBUTION PLANNED**:
  * Hook: 10-15 words (3 seconds)
  * Context: 15-25 words per scene
  * Information: 20-30 words per scene
  * CTA: 15-20 words (5-6 seconds)

- [ ] **BREATHING PAUSES PLANNED** - Natural pause points every 10-15 seconds

### 6. **Music & Audio Strategy**

- [ ] **MUSIC MOOD SELECTED** based on content tone:
  * Tense/Dramatic: Threats, warnings, urgent topics
  * Upbeat/Energetic: How-tos, success stories, solutions
  * Calm/Contemplative: Nature, education, explanations
  * Neutral/Documentary: Informational, factual content
  * Epic/Inspiring: Achievement, transformation, impact

- [ ] **AUDIO DYNAMICS PLANNED**:
  * Music volume low during narration (20-30%)
  * Music volume higher during visual-only moments (40-50%)
  * Music builds during emotional peaks
  * Music fades for critical information
  * Music supports but never competes with narration

- [ ] **SOUND EFFECTS CONSIDERATION**:
  * Transition whooshes (scene changes)
  * Emphasis sounds (statistics, reveals)
  * Ambient sounds (environment authenticity)
  * Keep minimal - don't overwhelm

### 7. **Platform-Specific Optimization**

- [ ] **ASPECT RATIO DETERMINED**:
  * YouTube Shorts: 9:16 (vertical)
  * TikTok: 9:16 (vertical)
  * Instagram Reels: 9:16 (vertical)
  * YouTube: 16:9 (horizontal)
  * Multi-platform: 1:1 (square) or 9:16 (vertical default)

- [ ] **PLATFORM CONSIDERATIONS APPLIED**:
  * **Vertical videos**: Subjects centered, text readable in small screen
  * **YouTube**: More detailed descriptions, chapter markers for long videos
  * **TikTok**: Faster pace, trending sounds consideration
  * **Instagram**: Visual aesthetics prioritized, caption-friendly

- [ ] **CAPTIONS STRATEGY**:
  * Always enabled for accessibility
  * Word-by-word highlighting for engagement
  * Key terms emphasized
  * Bottom-center placement (vertical) or bottom-third (horizontal)

### 8. **Call-to-Action Planning**

- [ ] **CTA TYPE SELECTED** based on article goal:
  * **Educational**: "Learn more at [website]"
  * **Warning/Alert**: "Check your [subject] today and report findings"
  * **How-to**: "Try this technique and share your results"
  * **Product/Service**: "Visit [website] for more information"
  * **Engagement**: "Comment your experience with [topic]"

- [ ] **CTA TIMING PLANNED**:
  * Final 5-6 seconds reserved for CTA
  * CTA mentioned in voiceover
  * CTA visible on screen (text overlay)
  * CTA reinforces core message

### 9. **Quality Gate - Relevance Check**

Before proceeding to Phase 3, verify script plan:

- [ ] **CORE MESSAGE ALIGNMENT** - Every planned scene relates to article's main topic
- [ ] **NO TOPIC DRIFT** - No scenes introduce unrelated information
- [ ] **FACT ACCURACY** - All planned content exists in source article
- [ ] **DURATION COMPLIANCE** - Scene count and word count match target duration
- [ ] **VISUAL FEASIBILITY** - All planned visuals are AI-searchable
- [ ] **NARRATIVE COHERENCE** - Scenes flow logically from hook to CTA

### 10. **Phase 2 Completion Verification**

- [ ] **SCENE STRUCTURE COMPLETE** - All scenes planned and mapped to content
- [ ] **VISUAL INVENTORY READY** - Each scene has specific visual description
- [ ] **WORD COUNT CALCULATED** - Target narration length confirmed
- [ ] **PLATFORM SPECS LOCKED** - Aspect ratio and platform features determined
- [ ] **CTA PLANNED** - Clear call-to-action finalized
- [ ] **RELEVANCE VERIFIED** - All content stays on-topic with article
- [ ] **PHASE 2 COMPLIANCE** - Ready to proceed to Phase 3

## INTEGRATED FALLBACK STRATEGIES

### **When Article Content is Limited**

**If article lacks sufficient detail for target duration:**

- [ ] **REDUCE DURATION** - Suggest shorter video (e.g., 60s instead of 90s)
- [ ] **FOCUS ON DEPTH** - Cover fewer points with more detail
- [ ] **USE REPETITION STRATEGICALLY** - Reinforce key message from multiple angles
- [ ] **EXPAND EXAMPLES** - Use longer visual sequences to illustrate points
- [ ] **NEVER PAD WITH OFF-TOPIC CONTENT** - Better to be short than drift

### **When Visuals are Difficult to Source**

**If specific visuals from article are not stockable:**

- [ ] **USE VISUAL METAPHORS** - Find analogous imagery that conveys same concept
- [ ] **EMPLOY GRAPHICS** - Suggest diagrams, animations, or text-on-screen
- [ ] **FOCUS ON RELATED VISUALS** - Show environment, context, or results instead
- [ ] **NOTE LIMITATIONS** - Flag difficult visuals in output notes
- [ ] **PROVIDE ALTERNATIVES** - Offer 2-3 visual options per challenging scene

### **When Article Tone Conflicts with Platform**

**If article is highly technical but target is TikTok:**

- [ ] **SIMPLIFY LANGUAGE** - Use analogies and everyday terms
- [ ] **FOCUS ON "WHY IT MATTERS"** - Emphasize impact over mechanism
- [ ] **USE VISUAL EXPLANATIONS** - Let visuals carry complex concepts
- [ ] **ADJUST PACING** - Faster delivery for engaging platforms
- [ ] **MAINTAIN ACCURACY** - Simplify but don't misrepresent

### **When Duration-Content Mismatch Occurs**

**If article content doesn't fit target duration:**

- [ ] **COMMUNICATE MISMATCH** - Flag in output if significant
- [ ] **SUGGEST OPTIMAL DURATION** - Recommend based on content analysis
- [ ] **PRIORITIZE RUTHLESSLY** - Focus on most impactful elements
- [ ] **OFFER MULTI-PART OPTION** - Suggest series for long articles
- [ ] **NEVER SACRIFICE QUALITY** - Better shorter and good than longer and diluted

### **When Niche Requires Specialized Knowledge**

**If article uses technical jargon or niche terminology:**

- [ ] **KEEP KEY TERMS** - Maintain important technical vocabulary
- [ ] **ADD CONTEXT** - Briefly explain specialized terms
- [ ] **USE ANALOGIES** - Compare to familiar concepts
- [ ] **VISUAL DEFINITIONS** - Show what terms mean rather than just saying
- [ ] **TRUST SOURCE MATERIAL** - Don't oversimplify to the point of inaccuracy
